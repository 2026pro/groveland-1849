The Journey

Seven days of country, played as one continuous game. Touch a day on the survey map — territory ahead of the company stays unexplored until you reach it. The greater secrets stay sealed until you are on the trail.

The Rules of the Game

Simple to learn on Day 0. Impossible to master alone.

  1. Every traveller chooses one historical character to live as for seven days.
  2. Every character receives one sealed secret at the threshold.
  3. Travellers receive 60 in-game gold tokens to build a loadout at the outfitter.
  4. Food, pack weight and equipment must be managed — the trail charges for every pound.
  5. Travellers choose clothing, a backpack, tools, a horse, a dog, a tent, a boat and a theatrical firearm prop.
  6. The survey map reveals new territory each day.
  7. Travellers must trade information and cooperate to advance.
  8. All ten secret fragments are needed to reach the final destination.
  9. All firearms are safe, inert theatrical props — no live weapons, ever.

The Company’s Assets
